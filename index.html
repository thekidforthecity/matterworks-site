SharePoint & M365 optimization for a mid-size UK law firm
A mid-size UK law firm was running their legal operations on a SharePoint environment that had grown organically over years - inconsistent document structures, poor metadata, and no real matter management to speak of. The team was spending significant time navigating the system rather than doing legal work. We audited their Microsoft 365 environment end-to-end, redesigned their SharePoint architecture, and built custom tooling on top of the ecosystem to bring document management, metadata, and matter management into a single coherent workflow.
The result: a 23% increase in operational efficiency - measured over three months post go-live - and a legal team that actually enjoys using their own systems.
